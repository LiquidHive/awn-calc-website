About AwnCalc

AwnCalc is a free pricing calculator built with Flutter for awning and shade component professionals. It uses intelligent formulas to compute prices for slats, flashings, tubes, and hardware — all stored locally on your device.

Whether you need to price a residential patio cover or a commercial shade structure, just add your measurements, adjust unit pricing, and get an instant job total with tax and interest. No internet required after download.

  • check_circle 15 pre-loaded components with smart formulas
  • check_circle Configurable tax rates, interest, and currency
  • check_circle Imperial or metric unit system
  • check_circle Light, dark, and system theme modes
  • check_circle All data stored locally — no accounts needed
